Precautions for Common Conditions

  • Fever: keep patient hydrated and monitor temperature every 4-6 hours.
  • Breathing trouble: keep patient upright and loosen tight clothing.
  • Chest pain: avoid physical activity and call emergency support immediately.
  • Vomiting or diarrhea: give ORS and fluids if patient is conscious.
  • Severe weakness/confusion: do not delay ambulance request.

About

LifeLink is a college project focused on fast emergency coordination between patients, admins, drivers, and ambulances in one connected platform.
